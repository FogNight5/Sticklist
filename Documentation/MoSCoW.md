###### Must have



* You can click on a button, causing an entry with the date and time of that moment
* You can add and delete multiple categories and track all of these seperatly

###### 

###### Should have



* You should be able to import and export your entries for any category
* You should be able to delete or add entries in the afterward

###### 

###### Could have



* You could be able to give any category a specific description
* You could be able to switch between light and dark mode
* You could be able to customize the design of the app
* You could be able to add a comment when making an entry

###### 

###### Wont have



* You won't be able to track your tally lists on multiple devices
* You won't be able to interact with other users in the app
* You won't be able to visualize your data by graphs or collect complex data
