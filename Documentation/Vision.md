###### What is Sticklist?



Sticklist is a telly list app that makes it easy to track how often and when different events happen.



For example, you can use Sticklist for tracking whether you did your daily task, how often, and when.



You can add multiple categories to track different events.



Tracking becomes easy by just opening the app and clicking on the button of the supposed category.



###### 

###### Who is Sticklist for?



Sticklist is primarily a project of my own, but it's designed to be used by anyone who needs an easy tally list with date and time.



Sticklist is entirely in English so that it can be used by most people. However, it's not planned to be translated, so basic English is a requirement.



###### 

###### What is Sticklist not supposed to be?



Sticklist can be used however you want. However, it's only designed to be a tally list with date and time.



All features must aim at being a tally list with date and time for different categories and nothing more.
